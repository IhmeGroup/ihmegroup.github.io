**Hannah Ramsperger**  
*Visiting Undergraduate Researcher*  

Hannah is a visiting undergraduate student from Caltech studying mechanical engineering with a minor in aerospace. Her interests lie in fluid dynamics and thermodynamics, particularly in combustion and propulsion applications. At the FX Lab, Hannah conducts computational modeling of the thermostructural response of porous-media burners, with the goal of improving the stability and performance of porous-burner matrix. In her free time, she enjoys tennis and skiing.
