**Muhammad Harchaoui**  
*Visiting Doctoral Scholar*\
Engineering degree, CentraleSupélec, 2024\
M.Sc. in Aeronautics and Space, Université Paris-Saclay, 2024

Visiting from the EM2C Laboratory at CNRS/CentraleSupélec, Muhammad's research focuses on numerical methods, subgrid-scale modeling, and machine learning for particle-laden turbulent flows. At the FX Lab, he works on combining lattice Boltzmann methods and machine learning approaches to improve two-way coupling models for fluid–particle interactions in large-eddy simulations. Outside research, he enjoys chess, strategy games, and going to the gym.
