**Jan Wilhelm Gärtner**  
*Visiting Postdoctoral Scholar*\
Ph.D. University of Stuttgart, 2024\
M. Sc. University of Stuttgart, 2018\
B. Sc. University of Stuttgart, 2015

Jan’s primary research focuses on the simulation of compressible, reactive, multiphase flows under extreme conditions. His work includes modeling flashing cryogenic sprays during the ignition of upper-stage rocket engines, as well as wall heat transfer in expander cycle engines. He is also involved in developing advanced probability density function (PDF) methods for spray combustion, particularly using the Multiple Mapping Conditioning (MMC) approach. Outside the lab, Jan is a passionate endurance athlete pursuing the goal of completing an Ironman-distance triathlon.
