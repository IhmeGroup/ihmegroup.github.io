**Timoteo Dinelli**  
*Postdoctoral Scholar, Mechanical Engineering*  
Ph.D. Politecnico di Milano, 2026\
M.S. Politecnico di Milano, 2021  
B.S. Politecnico di Milano, 2019

Timoteo's research focuses on reaction kinetics in supercritical fluids and ultrafast chemistry, alongside data assimilation methods in chemical kinetic dynamical systems. He builds data-driven frameworks that couple experimental and synthetic measurements with detailed kinetic models, developing predictive, uncertainty-aware models to improve combustion and pyrolysis predictions for conventional and renewable fuels. Outside of lab, he's pro-pizza in all its forms — pineapple included — and enjoys rock climbing.
